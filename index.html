Backward/Forward Sweep (BFS) Mathematical Solver Reference

1. Backward Sweep (Currents)

Traversing from leaf nodes to substation:

I_m = ((P_m - jQ_m) / V_m)*

Branch current equals downstream bus loads aggregated iteratively.

2. Forward Sweep (Voltages)

Traversing from substation to terminal buses:

V_m = V_k - I_km · (R_km + jX_km)

Nodal voltages updated from predecessor bus voltage drop.

3. Convergence Criterion

Iterative maximum absolute voltage variation:

max |V^(iter+1) - V^(iter)| < 1e-5

Tolerates high R/X radial feeders with zero ill-conditioning.
